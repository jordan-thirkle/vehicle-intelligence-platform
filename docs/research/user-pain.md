# User Pain → Product Response

## “Why am I paying for information government already gives me?”
Response: keep government-derived basics accessible and provide direct official links. Monetise added intelligence, licensed data and workflow value.

## “Two reports disagree. Which one is right?”
Response: preserve source-level evidence and show conflicts instead of collapsing them into a false green tick.

## “No record” gets interpreted as “definitely clean.”
Response: enforce `CLEAR`, `UNKNOWN`, `FLAGGED`. Missing/unsupported evidence resolves to UNKNOWN.

## “My report/credit expired.”
Response: Vehicle Passport persists. Individual checks show freshness and can be refreshed.

## “A history report tells me the past, not what will break next.”
Response: combine age/mileage/model evidence with maintenance state and clearly label statistical/estimated risk separately from known facts.

## “I have a fault code but don't know what to do.”
Response: exact-vehicle diagnostic session ranks hypotheses and asks for the next discriminating evidence rather than dumping generic causes.

## “The app wants me to buy its scanner.”
Response: hardware-neutral OBD strategy; support standards/imports and charge for intelligence rather than unnecessary hardware lock-in.

## “The parts catalogue says it fits, but my car has a different part.”
Response: separate factory candidate from current installed component and support physical label/photo verification.

## “AI confidently gave me the wrong technical specification.”
Response: provenance is visible; safety-critical specifications require appropriate technical authority and cannot be promoted from unsupported AI inference.

## “I maintained it, but I can't prove what was done.”
Response: provide a permanent ownership timeline for maintenance, repairs, inspections, modifications and diagnostic outcomes. Preserve useful owner-entered history while distinguishing it from invoice-evidenced, garage-verified and authoritative records.

The evidence-level distinction is essential: self-maintained owners need continuity, while future buyers need protection from a user simply typing “full service history.”
