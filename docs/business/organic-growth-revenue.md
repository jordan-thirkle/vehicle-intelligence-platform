# Organic growth and revenue system

## Principle

Vehicle Intelligence should compound through useful, evidence-led pages and persistent product value—not bulk keyword permutations. Every indexable page must answer a distinct user decision and remain useful without conversion.

## Acquisition architecture

1. **Transactional foundations:** free vehicle check, MOT history, Vehicle Passport and provider comparison.
2. **Diagnostic problem clusters:** one substantial guide per validated symptom pattern, linking symptoms to safe evidence-gathering steps rather than declaring a failed component.
3. **Ownership clusters:** MOT advisory interpretation, maintenance evidence, installed-component identification and preparing a trustworthy sale record.
4. **Vehicle-specific pages:** publish only where physical/reference evidence adds original value. Do not generate make/model permutations from thin templates.
5. **Comparison updates:** use the public-page monitor to review prices and claims; never publish unattended competitive changes.

## Monetisation ladder

| Stage | User value | Revenue mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Free discovery | Registration validation, official routing and evidence education | Organic acquisition |
| Saved Passport | Persistent ownership record across devices | Free account; activation and retention |
| Passport Plus | Evidence storage, reminders, Mechanical DNA and diagnostic sessions | Monthly/annual subscription after secure checkout exists |
| Licensed check | Finance, theft, write-off, salvage and valuation data | Transparent provider fee or commission, subject to contract |
| Professional verification | Garage-confirmed components, work and outcomes | Garage tools, verification fee or professional subscription |

## Conversion measurement

Measure page intent → lookup outcome → Passport demo → saved Passport → evidence added → retained owner → paid upgrade. Never send VRMs, VINs, passwords or free-text evidence through marketing analytics.

## Publication gate

A new SEO page ships only when it has a named intent, original evidence or analysis, appropriate sources, a human review date, internal links and a useful next action. Pages that cannot meet that bar remain drafts or `noindex`.

## Revenue prerequisites

Subscriptions cannot be sold until authentication, consent records, cloud persistence, privacy/terms, support and secure payment processing are live. Licensed history checks additionally require a provider agreement and exact guarantee/coverage wording. Revenue is an outcome to validate, not a promise of passive income.
