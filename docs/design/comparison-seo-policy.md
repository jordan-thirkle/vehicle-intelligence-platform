# Comparison SEO policy

Comparison content exists to help a buyer make a decision, not merely to capture a competitor-name query.

## Publication rules

1. Prefer one comprehensive comparison hub over near-duplicate “alternative” pages.
2. Compare products serving the same decision and use material, objective features.
3. Link each named factual claim to a provider-owned source and show the review date.
4. Do not use competitor logos, imitate their visual identity, imply affiliation, denigrate them or claim superiority without appropriate evidence.
5. State important asymmetries clearly. Until Vehicle Intelligence has a licensed provider, it is not a replacement for finance, theft, write-off or salvage checks.
6. Prices, guarantees and coverage are volatile. Review them before publication and after the competitive monitor flags change.
7. Every page must add distinct decision value and be part of normal site navigation. Do not programmatically produce brand, model or location permutations.
8. A human reviews all comparative claims before deployment. Legal advice should be obtained before running paid comparative advertising or making strong superiority claims.

## Expansion gate

A dedicated page such as “Vehicle Intelligence vs HPI” is justified only when:

- search or customer evidence shows distinct demand;
- Vehicle Intelligence is a live, comparable product;
- the page can offer substantial unique analysis;
- every objective claim has current substantiation;
- the page receives a scheduled review owner and date.

Until those conditions are met, the maintained comparison hub is the canonical destination.
