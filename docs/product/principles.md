# Product Principles

1. **Evidence over assertion** — show why the platform believes something.
2. **Unknown is not clear** — absence of data is not evidence of absence.
3. **Public information stays accessible** — do not manufacture a paywall around freely available government information.
4. **Link to authority** — official GOV.UK/OEM verification remains available even when data is integrated.
5. **Permanent Passport, refreshable evidence** — the user's vehicle record persists; time-sensitive evidence carries freshness.
6. **Factory is not current** — replacement/modification history matters.
7. **Verification beats confidence theatre** — physical labels and authoritative records are qualitatively different from probabilistic inference.
8. **Diagnose by reducing uncertainty** — rank hypotheses and seek discriminating evidence.
9. **Safety before engagement** — unsafe driving/repair states override product gamification or conversion goals.
10. **Hardware neutral** — support standards and imports rather than creating unnecessary dongle lock-in.
11. **Source technical specifications appropriately** — safety-critical procedures, torque values and limits require suitable authority/licensing.
12. **Commercial neutrality of truth** — affiliates/sponsors cannot alter fitment, safety or diagnostic ranking.
13. **Privacy by design** — do not expose keeper identity; minimise personal data; obtain explicit consent before using owner-generated repair outcomes for aggregate learning.
14. **International by adapters, UK by launch** — country-specific authorities sit behind provider contracts rather than leaking into the domain model.
