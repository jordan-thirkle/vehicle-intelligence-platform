# Product Vision

## Mission

Help people understand the vehicle in front of them using evidence they can inspect and verify.

## The problem

Vehicle information is fragmented across government services, history-check vendors, OEM documents, parts catalogues, workshop-data products, scan tools, invoices and the physical car. Existing products often optimise for one moment—usually purchase—rather than the vehicle's entire ownership lifecycle.

## Product

Vehicle Intelligence Platform creates a persistent **Vehicle Passport / Digital Twin** that combines:
- authoritative records;
- factory/candidate specification;
- current physically verified components;
- MOT/maintenance/repair history;
- symptoms, DTCs and live observations;
- diagnostic hypotheses and outcomes;
- evidence provenance and confidence.

## Lifecycle

`Discover → Verify → Inspect → Buy → Own → Maintain → Diagnose → Repair → Verify outcome`

## Wedge

UK-first. The acquisition surface is useful free vehicle identification and official-service routing. The differentiated product is the evidence graph, Mechanical DNA, current-vehicle verification and diagnostic reasoning.

## Long-term moat

With explicit privacy/consent controls, structured resolved outcomes can create a real-world fault graph:

`vehicle configuration + symptoms + DTC/live evidence + inspection → repair → outcome`

The value is not merely possessing public records; it is learning which evidence discriminates faults and which repairs actually resolved them.
